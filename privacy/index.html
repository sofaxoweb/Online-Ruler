Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information TrueRuler ("we", "our") collects when you use trueruleronline.com, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

The ruler works entirely in your browser. Your calibration (pixels-per-inch), unit choice and theme are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data is never sent to our servers, never shared, and you can delete it at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security and to keep the service running. We do not use this data to identify you.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

4. Third-party links

Our pages may link to external sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please review their policies separately.

5. Children's privacy

TrueRuler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision.

7. Contact
